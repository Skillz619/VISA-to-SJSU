The instructions for this Canvas assignment are for an F1 student to correctly submit a CPT approval request. Use the instructions in this assignment to complete and submit the ISSS eForm for your CPT request. Here are the steps and instructions for submitting a CPT request:

[Student]

Step 1. Completes the iSpartan eForm. Note that there are two files to upload using the iSpartan eForm described in steps 2 and 3 below. In the iSpartan eForm, for department advisor enter Dan Harkey if you are an MS SE student, Haonan Wang if you are an MS CMPE student, and Magdalini Eirinaki if you are MS AI student. Enter the following email address to route your CPT request for processing. Do not enter your advisor's email address.

     sjsu-cmpe-isss-eforms-group@sjsu.edu 


Step 2. Uploads the official Employment Offer Letter and select it in the iSpartan eForm under the heading Upload CPT Employment Offer Letter.

Note the following information from ISSS about your offer letter:

If the offer is remote, then this should be indicated on the letter, including the address where the student will be working.
The letter needs to include the following information:
Must be printed on company's letterhead
Must be dated within the last 3 months
Include job title
Include job description
Include employment start and end date
Include physical address where you will work (street number and name, city, state postal code, and suite/building number)
Include number of hours per week
Step 3. Creates the files highlighted in bold text described below and combines them into one PDF file to attach to the application. Attach this single PDF file under the iSpartan eForm heading Upload Document Required By Your Department:

3.1. Scan your admission letter (front and back pages)                     

3.2. Create your unofficial transcript PDF file                   

3.3. Create a screen capture of your most updated MySJSU Course History as a PDF file                     

3.4. (If applicable) If you were admitted conditionally classified and met admission conditions by passing a subject test or taking a class outside of SJSU, create a PDF file showing the proof for completing your admission prerequisites.

3.5. Download and complete the following Program of Study (POS) template for your degree program:

MSAI:          MSAI-LastName-FirstName-POS-v1.docx Download MSAI-LastName-FirstName-POS-v1.docx 
MSCMPE:   MSCMPE-LastName-FirstName-POS-v1.docx Download MSCMPE-LastName-FirstName-POS-v1.docx
MSSE:          MSSE-LastName-FirstName-POS-v1.docx Download MSSE-LastName-FirstName-POS-v1.docx

[Advisor]

Step 4.     Receives iSpartan eform request, reviews, and approves the request if everything required is accurate and complete. Request form goes to ISSS next for their review and approval.

[Student]

Step 5.     Receives CPT approval from ISSS.
